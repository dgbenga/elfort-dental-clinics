# Google Stitch Visual Mockup Protocol: Home-Visit Dentist

## 1. Project Visual Overview

- **Product Mission:** Modern, high-conversion landing page for a friendly, licensed solo dentist providing home-visit and workplace dental care.
- **Core User Persona:** Stressed caregivers, seniors, shift workers, and nervous patients seeking unhurried, gentle dental care in the comfort of their home.
- **Locked Palette:**
  - Dominant Background Surface (60%): Very Light Sky Blue (`#EAF3FF`)
  - Elevated Content Cards & Header (30%): Crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Primary Action CTA & Headings (10%): Deep Authority Navy (`#0B3A75`)
  - Body Text: Muted Navy Ink (`#2F4562`)
  - Dividing Lines & Borders: Soft Sky Line (`#C7DCF3`)
  - Accent / Hover Surface: Soft Sky Deep (`#DCEBFC`)
- **Atmosphere & Density:** Clean, calm, medical-grade hygiene and safety reassurance. Spacious layout with 16px soft-radius cards, zero visual clutter, and commanding typographic hierarchy without badge pills.

---

## 2. Deterministic Google Stitch Prompt (Core Hero & Value Proposition Viewport)

```text
Flat 2D desktop application screenshot, 16:9 widescreen, 1920x1080 resolution, direct front-facing view, NO 3D perspective tilt, NO laptop mockup frame, NO claymockups.

Zone 1 (Top Header Bar): Height 80px, background pure white #FFFFFF with subtle bottom border #C7DCF3. Left side features clean bold typographic logo "Dr. Dentist · Home Dental Care" in deep navy #0B3A75. Center shows horizontal text links: "How It Works", "Who We Help", "Services & Prices", "Meet Dentist", "FAQ" in muted navy #2F4562. Right side features a secondary outline phone button "Call: 07000 000000" and a primary solid deep navy #0B3A75 button "Message Us" with white text.

Zone 2 (Hero Conversion Viewport): Full width, background soft very light sky blue #EAF3FF, padding top 96px, padding bottom 96px. Center-left column displays an extra-large commanding headline "Dental care that comes to you." in bold deep navy #0B3A75, with no badge pills, no tags, and no icons above the heading. Below the headline is a generous, highly readable lead paragraph: "A friendly, licensed dentist visits your home or workplace for check-ups, cleanings, and simple repairs. Clear prices before we begin, and zero pressure." in muted navy ink #2F4562. Below the copy is a large primary button with solid deep navy #0B3A75 background, rounded 16px corners, 54px height, displaying white text "Message Us on WhatsApp" next to a white messaging icon. Under the button is small muted micro-text: "Free to ask · No forms · No obligation · We usually reply within 30 minutes".

Zone 3 (Trust Proof Cards Bar): Positioned directly below the hero buttons, a horizontal row of 3 crisp white #FFFFFF cards with 16px rounded corners, thin light blue borders #C7DCF3, and subtle elevation shadows. Card 1 contains a blue outline checkmark icon with text "Fully Licensed Solo Dentist". Card 2 contains a blue outline shield icon with text "Sterile, Single-Use Instruments". Card 3 contains a blue outline price tag icon with text "Clear Price Before We Start".

Zone 4 (Right Visual Area): A warm, professional, authentic portrait of a friendly dentist in clean medical scrubs smiling in a warm, tidy residential living room setting, soft natural window lighting, no luxury penthouses, no clinical vinyl chairs.

STRICT NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS: NO analytics charts, NO line graphs, NO bar charts, NO floating decorative bubbles, NO colorful gradients, NO neon green buttons, NO rounded badge pills with sparkles above headings, NO mobile frames, clean minimalist professional medical interface only.
```
